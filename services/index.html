How a project runs

  1. 1

    Brief

    We talk through the goal, the audience and what already exists. I come back with questions, scope and a proposal.

  2. 2

    Direction

    One or two clear directions, shown on real applications rather than a logo on a white page.

  3. 3

    Design

    The chosen direction built into a full system, with your feedback at set checkpoints.

  4. 4

    Handoff

    Final files, templates and guidance your team can use from day one.

Ways to work together

  • A defined project

    A new identity, a campaign, a guidelines document or a deck, with a clear scope and end date.

  • Ongoing design support

    A set amount of design time each month for teams that need steady, on-brand output.

  • A review or consultation

    A focused session or written audit when you need direction rather than production.
