Aureyaأوريا

Brand world and social campaign

Brand identityCampaignsSocial mediaPackaging

A bilingual brand world and weekly social system for a luxury skincare house in Saudi Arabia.

Aureya is a skincare house for the modern Gulf woman: rooted in ritual, quiet in its confidence, fluent in two languages. The brief was the complete visual world, from identity and product architecture to art direction, a campaign and a social system that can run every week without losing its poise.

The idea is golden hour, kept. The palette is the product line: three colours for three collections, so a customer can read the shelf and the feed by colour alone. Arabic leads, in one weight and without stretched kashida.
