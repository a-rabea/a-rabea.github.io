Brand identity and mascot

Overview

A loud, fast pizza brand rebuilt from an old project of mine, with a mascot called Rushu.

Scope
Wordmark, brandmark, mascot, packaging, app, social, out-of-home
Type
Arabic wordmark customised from Marhey, with Readex Pro and Bricolage Grotesque
The challenge
Rebuild one of my earliest identities into a system loud and fast enough for delivery, the storefront and the feed.
My role
Art direction and design.
Creative direction
A customised Arabic wordmark, a simple brandmark, and Rushu, a running slice who carries the tone of voice.
Deliverables
Wordmark and brandmark, mascot and poses, colour and type toolkit, menu, packaging, delivery, storefront, app, social, out-of-home and campaign.
Read the full write-up

Pizza Rush started as one of my earliest portfolio pieces. In 2026 I took it apart and rebuilt it, and the case study shows that jump from version one to version two.

The new system runs on a customised Arabic wordmark, a brandmark, and Rushu, a running slice who carries the tone of voice. It holds together across menu, box, delivery bag, storefront, app, social and out-of-home.
