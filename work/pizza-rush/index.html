Pizza Rushبيتزا رش

Brand identity and mascot

Brand identityMascotPackagingApp and digitalCampaigns

A loud, fast pizza brand rebuilt from an old project of mine, with a mascot called Rushu.

Pizza Rush started as one of my earliest portfolio pieces. In 2026 I took it apart and rebuilt it, and the case study shows that jump from version one to version two.

The new system runs on a customised Arabic wordmark, a brandmark, and Rushu, a running slice who carries the tone of voice. It holds together across menu, box, delivery bag, storefront, app, social and out-of-home.
