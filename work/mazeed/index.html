Client work

Visual direction and design system for a B2B accounting platform serving 1,000+ clients across the UAE and KSA.

mazeed sells accounting, tax and e-invoicing software to small and mid-size businesses in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. It had to look as trustworthy as a bank and move as fast as a startup.

I inherited the wordmark and the core colours. My job was everything around them: how the purple, the mint and the chevron behave across hundreds of assets in two scripts. That meant a creative direction for 2026 built on a competitive read of QuickBooks, Xero and Zoho Books, one layout for three products, a 16-icon service system, and bilingual ads that are mirrored for right-to-left reading rather than translated. Single campaign systems reached 80 assets across two languages and five formats.
