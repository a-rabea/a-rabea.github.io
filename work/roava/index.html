A specialty coffee brand concept that is calm and loud at once: soft lavender, broken by one lime accent.

The challenge

A self-set brief: a specialty coffee brand that feels calm and loud at once.

Creative direction

The answer was a system of restraint. Three soft purples do the quiet work, ink carries the type, and lime appears once per composition, never twice.

The system

The rounded wordmark hides a coffee bean inside the o, with three strokes of steam above it. An arcade pattern borrowed from the shopfront is built from one module and always rises from the bottom edge, across cups, pouches, bags, signage and the feed.
