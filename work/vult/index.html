VULTڤولت

The ideaIn Arabic, jahd (جهد) means both electrical voltage and human effort.

Brand identity and packaging Self-initiated

Brand identityCustom letteringPackagingCampaigns

An Arabic identity concept for an energy drink named after a unit of voltage.

The challenge

A self-set brief: an energy drink identity that works first in Arabic and could only belong to this name, from the can to the poster to the feed.

Creative direction

In Arabic, jahd (جهد) means both electrical voltage and human effort. VULT sits exactly between the two, and the whole identity is built on that coincidence.

The system

Instead of a Latin wordmark with a gradient, the identity is built on an Arabic wordmark: four letters carried on a single wire, with a spark drawn into the lam. Six flat colours, no gradients, and a set of single-word Arabic headlines carry it from can to poster to the feed. The case study itself is written in Arabic, on purpose.
