VULTڤولت

Brand identity and packaging

Brand identityCustom letteringPackagingCampaigns

An Arabic identity for an energy drink named after a unit of voltage.

In Arabic, jahd (جهد) means both electrical voltage and human effort. VULT sits exactly between the two, and the whole identity is built on that coincidence.

The old Latin wordmark with its gradient was replaced by an Arabic wordmark: four letters carried on a single wire, with a spark drawn into the lam. Six flat colours, no gradients, and a set of single-word Arabic headlines carry it from can to poster to the feed. The case study itself is written in Arabic, on purpose.
