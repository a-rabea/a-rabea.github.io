AYWAأيوة

The ideaThe identity runs on one rule, the Lean: everything tilts 10° forward.

Brand identity for a fintech app Self-initiated

Brand identityApp and digitalCampaignsSocial media

An identity concept for a card and money app aimed at Egypt's 16 to 28 year olds. Aywa is the loud Egyptian yes.

The challenge

A self-set brief: an identity for a Gen Z money app that feels as quick and direct as the product it imagines, with four minutes to open an account and one tap to pay, send or split a bill.

Creative direction

The idea is money in colour. Your money splits into five pockets, and the colours from the photography become those pockets: pink for going out, volt for main, orange for food, blue for saving and chrome for bills. Each pocket gets its own virtual card, so you always see where your money goes.

The system

In the concept, AYWA opens an account in four minutes with no branch, then lets you tap to pay, send in seconds and split a bill in one tap. The identity runs on one rule, the Lean: everything tilts 10° forward. The custom wordmark has no vertical strokes, and the centre of the W breaks the cap line by 18%. That peak becomes the app symbol and the hero of the card.
