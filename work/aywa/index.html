The challenge
A card and money app for Egypt's 16 to 28 year olds needed an identity as loud and simple as the word itself.
My role
Brand identity and art direction.
Creative direction
The Lean: everything tilts 10° forward. Money in colour: five pockets, five colours, five virtual cards.
Deliverables
Custom wordmark and symbol, colour and type system, physical and virtual cards, app and onboarding, campaign and out-of-home, social and website.
Read the full write-up

AYWA opens an account in four minutes with no branch, then lets you tap to pay, send in seconds and split a bill in one tap. The identity runs on one rule, the Lean: everything tilts 10° forward. The custom wordmark has no vertical strokes, and the centre of the W breaks the cap line by 18%. That peak becomes the app symbol and the hero of the card.

The idea is money in colour. Your money splits into five pockets, and the colours from the photography become those pockets: pink for going out, volt for main, orange for food, blue for saving and chrome for bills. Each pocket gets its own virtual card, so you always see where your money goes.
