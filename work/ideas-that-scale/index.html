Social mediaCampaigns

Social systems for nine brands: one idea, a few hard rules, scaled across formats, languages and markets.

Context

The first half is shipped work for mazeed, Bayantech, ATOP, Aureya and Reefer Zone, from a single SaaS idea built once and shipped in UAE English, UAE Arabic and Saudi Arabic, to refrigeration posts where the spec a buyer cares about is the headline. The second half is the ideas: four campaigns built on bilingual wordplay and two brands made from scratch.

The challenge

Keep nine brands recognisable across three markets, two languages and every format, at weekly volume, without each post turning into a one-off.

The system

I treat a feed like a brand system. One idea, a few hard rules, then scale it across formats, languages and markets without losing the idea.
