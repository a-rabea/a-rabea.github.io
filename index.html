About

I design brands that have to work in two scripts at once. Arabic is never a translation tag under the English: it gets its own typography, its own reading direction and its own voice.

For more than five years I have worked across B2B SaaS, language services, healthcare and construction. I currently design for ELIXIR MD, where I wrote the 68-page brand guidelines and the Arabic typography system that extends them. Before that I led the visual direction at mazeed, and at Bayantech I led a team of five designers across five brands at once.

How I work

Systems before assets
One idea, a few hard rules, then scale. At mazeed a single campaign system reached 80 assets across two languages and five formats.
Arabic-first, not Arabic-also
English, UAE Arabic and Saudi Arabic each get their own layout, mirrored for reading direction rather than translated in place.
Creative operations
Feedback, QA and asset management that let a team ship 250+ designs a month. I built CreativeVault, which cut file retrieval time by 90%.
