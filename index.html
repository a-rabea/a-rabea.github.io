What I do

Five services, and three smaller ways to start. See services and how I work

  • Brand identityA logo and a visual system with clear rules, so your brand looks the same in every hand that uses it.
  • Social media and campaignsSocial media design and campaigns built on one strong idea, so every post, story and ad looks like it came from the same brand.
  • Brand guidelinesGuidelines your team will actually open: foundations, usage rules and templates, written so the next designer needs no briefing.
  • Pitch and presentation designDecks that make dense information readable in seconds, for investors, leadership, tenders and sales.
  • Creative direction and consultingA senior eye on your brand: where it stands, where it should go, and how your team gets it there.

I care most about the hundredth asset: the post, deck or campaign that ships a year after launch and still looks like it came from the same place.

I'm a senior brand designer and art director in Cairo with more than five years in B2B SaaS, language services, healthcare and construction. I've led visual direction for an accounting platform in the UAE and KSA, run creative for five brands at once with a team of five designers, and built the guidelines, QA and asset systems that keep a brand consistent at volume.
