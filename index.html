About

A brand is judged by its hundredth asset, not its first. I design the system behind it, so every post, deck and campaign after launch still looks like it came from the same place.

I'm a brand designer and art director in Cairo with more than five years across B2B SaaS, healthcare, language services and consumer brands. I've run creative for five brands at once, led a team of designers, and built the guidelines and workflows that keep a brand consistent at volume. Alongside that, I take on a small number of independent projects.
